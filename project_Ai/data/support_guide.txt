
ACCOUNT ACCESS

Customers can reset their password from the Account Settings page. They must select Security and then choose Change Password.

If a customer cannot sign in, they can select Forgot Password on the login page. A password-reset link will be sent to the email address registered with the account. The link expires after 30 minutes.

After five unsuccessful login attempts, the account is temporarily locked for 15 minutes. Customer-support agents cannot view or provide a customer's password.


REFUND POLICY

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase. The request must include the order number and the email address used for the purchase.

Refunds are returned to the original payment method. After approval, processing normally takes five to seven business days.

Digital products that have been completely downloaded and customized products are not eligible for refunds unless they are defective.


ORDER CANCELLATION

An order may be cancelled before it has been shipped. Customers can open the Orders page, select the order, and choose Cancel Order.

If the order has already shipped, it cannot be cancelled. The customer must wait for delivery and then follow the return process.


CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Customer support is available Monday through Friday from 9 AM to 6 PM. Support is unavailable on public holidays.

Customers should include their order number when contacting support about purchases. Passwords, payment-card numbers, and other sensitive information must never be included in a support message.
